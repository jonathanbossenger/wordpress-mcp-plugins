      How the ranking works

      • Every check and standard task scores Pass 1, Partial ½, Fail 0, multiplied by the weight of its group. Unknown and not-applicable checks are left out.
      • The score is the share of possible points, out of 100. Ties go to the plugin with more active installs.
      • "100+ installs" plugins were chosen by size. "Newer" plugins have fewer than 100 installs and were hand-picked from a pre-screen of their readme, ratings and update history.
