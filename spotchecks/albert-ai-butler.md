# albert-ai-butler spot-check

Version checked: 1.5.0. Verdict: The review holds up. Every Pass I was asked to check stands up against the source, with only minor caveats that do not change any status.

## Other findings
- Free logging only records abilities that go through Albert's `BaseAbility::guarded_execute` or `ToolCallObserver`. "Every ability call is logged" may overstate coverage for third-party and `core/` abilities reached through execute-ability. This does not change the Partial.
- Removing a user from "Who may connect" revokes their tokens (`src/Admin/Connections.php:478`). Token validation itself does not re-check the allowed list.
