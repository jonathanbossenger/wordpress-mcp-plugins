## Other findings
- The review says only /ping and /health are public. There are also /detached/run and /self-update/run (POST, single-use sha256-hashed token with a TTL), /self-update/health and /connection-check-challenge. None of them exposes tools.
- Bulk ops: the approval gate is based on irreversibility, not count (`includes/cli/trait-wpvibe-cli-security.php:31-37`). A trash of many posts runs with no confirmation at all.
- The approval bypass accepts proofs only on /cli/run-approved and /code-snippet, and the site's own setting is re-checked when the op runs (`includes/class-wpvibe-op-proof-v2.php:21-24`, `includes/class-wpvibe-op-proof-v2.php:58-63`). This supports the SAFE-CONFIRM Pass.
